中文

我们是谁

「去过哪儿」是一款记录登机牌、火车票和自驾行程，并在地图上查看足迹的 iOS 应用。本政策说明应用会使用哪些信息、用在哪里，以及你如何删除它们。

我们处理的信息

  • 你录入的行程。航班号、车次、车站与机场、时间、座位、舱位、票价、备注，以及你拍摄或选入的票根图片。这些内容保存在你的设备上。
  • 相机。仅在你主动拍摄票根时使用，用来识别票面文字。识别在设备本地完成。
  • 照片。在你授权后，用于选择票根图片，或根据照片自带的拍摄位置整理旅途相册。你也可以选择把示例卡片保存到相册。照片匹配在设备本地完成，我们不会把相册上传到自己的服务器。
  • 位置。仅在使用期间获取。用于在迷雾地图中点亮你所在的区县，以及记录自驾时填入出发地或目的地。桌面小组件打卡也会使用同一项定位权限。
  • 通知与实时活动。在你开启后，于本机发送值机、登机和起降提醒，并在锁屏与灵动岛显示航班进度。这些提醒由系统在本机触发。
  • iCloud。若设备已登录 iCloud，行程数据可能通过 Apple CloudKit 在你自己的设备之间同步。我们没有单独的账号系统。

何时会离开你的设备

  • Apple 地图。搜索地点、逆地理编码和自驾路线由 Apple MapKit 完成。
  • 航班航迹。只有在你查看或刷新某趟航班的在线航迹时，应用才会把该航班呼号发送给 adsb.lol 与 adsb.fi，用于查询公开的 ADS-B 航迹。不会附带你的姓名、照片或通讯录。
  • iCloud 同步。如上所述，仅在你的 Apple 账号内同步你自己的行程。

没有网络时，地图足迹、离线航线推算和已保存的票根仍可在本机使用。

我们不会做的事

  • 不要求注册账号，不收集手机号或通讯录。
  • 不投放广告，不出售个人信息。
  • 不把相册、定位或票根图片上传到开发者自己的服务器。

保存与删除

行程和票根图片保存在本机，直到你在应用里删除对应票据，或卸载应用。若曾通过 iCloud 同步，也可在系统的 iCloud 设置中管理这些数据。关闭相机、照片、定位或通知权限，可在 iOS「设置」中随时完成。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会明知地收集儿童的个人信息。

政策更新

若本政策有实质变更，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期。继续使用应用即表示你了解更新后的政策。

English

Who we are

BeenThere（去过哪儿）is an iOS app for keeping boarding passes, train tickets, and road trips, and for seeing those trips on a map. This policy explains what the app uses, where it goes, and how you can delete it.

Information we process

  • Trips you enter.Flight or train numbers, stations and airports, times, seats, cabin, fare, notes, and ticket photos you take or choose. These stay on your device.
  • Camera.Used only when you photograph a ticket. Text recognition runs on the device.
  • Photos.With your permission, the app can import a ticket image or group past trip photos by the location stored in the photo. You may also save a sample card into your library. Matching happens on the device. We do not upload your photo library to a server we operate.
  • Location.Used only while the app or its widget is in use: to light the district you are in on the fog map, and to fill in a drive’s start or destination.
  • Notifications and Live Activities.If you turn them on, check-in, boarding, and arrival alerts are scheduled on the device, and flight progress can appear on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
  • iCloud.If you are signed in to iCloud, trip data may sync across your own devices through Apple CloudKit. There is no separate BeenThere account.

When information leaves your device

  • Apple Maps.Place search, reverse geocoding, and driving directions use Apple MapKit.
  • Flight tracks.Only when you open or refresh a flight’s live track, the flight callsign is sent to adsb.lol and adsb.fi to look up public ADS-B data. Your name, photos, and contacts are not sent.
  • iCloud.Sync stays inside your Apple account, as described above.

Saved tickets, the footprint map, and offline route estimates keep working without a network connection.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your phone number or contacts.
  • We do not show ads or sell personal information.
  • We do not upload your photo library, location history, or ticket images to a developer-operated server.

Retention and deletion

Trips and ticket images remain on the device until you delete the ticket in the app or delete the app. Data that synced through iCloud can also be managed in iCloud settings. Camera, Photos, Location, and Notifications can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

BeenThere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
